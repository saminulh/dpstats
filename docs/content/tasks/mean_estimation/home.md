[The task](https://saminulh.github.io/dpstats/mean_estimation/) is to privately estimate the mean of a dataset of sensitive records. An algorithm's error is the Euclidean distance from its estimate to the sample mean, the nonprivate estimate used in practice. It succeeds when that error is within a factor of τ of the sample mean's own sampling error. [The rankings](https://saminulh.github.io/dpstats/methodology/) order algorithms by τ@50, their median error ratio, lower is better.
