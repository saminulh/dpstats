[The task](https://saminulh.github.io/dpstats/single_coefficient_estimation/) is to privately estimate one designated regression coefficient from a dataset of sensitive features and outcomes. An algorithm's error is how far its estimate lies from the ordinary least-squares coefficient, the nonprivate estimate used in practice. It succeeds when that error is within a factor of τ of the nonprivate estimate's own standard error. [The rankings](https://saminulh.github.io/dpstats/methodology/) order algorithms by τ@50, their median error ratio, lower is better.
